## Sections that require real evidence rather than generated imagery

- Social proof: real customer portraits, approved logos, testimonial screenshots, and measured outcomes.
- TeraChat video demo: record an actual 20–30 second Android flow for motion proof; the static screenshots are now covered.
- Pricing: build as HTML/CSS, not as a raster image.
- Comparison table and FAQ: build as HTML/CSS for accessibility and mobile responsiveness.

## Prompt direction used

- Premium Malaysian SaaS marketing visuals.
- Terasetia presented as a complete WhatsApp sales operating system.
- Locally authentic Malaysian business owners and workspaces.
- Bright emerald/mint visuals for product sections, with a darker final CTA.
- No third-party logos, no fake testimonials, no customer personal data, and no fabricated performance claims.

For assets 15–20, image editing was limited to cropping, clarity and anonymization. The prompts explicitly preserved the authentic interface and prohibited invented features, UI labels, account data, device frames or marketing claims.
